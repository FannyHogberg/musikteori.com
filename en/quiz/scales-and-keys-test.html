Quiz / Final Test: Scales and Keys

Final Test: Scales and Keys

Whole steps, half steps, major, minor, key signatures and the circle of fifths.

About this test

This test concludes the course Scales and Keys. You will get questions about whole steps and half steps, the pattern of steps that builds the major scale and the natural minor scale, which notes belong to C major and A minor, and which keys are relative keys. You will also be tested on key signatures, the order in which sharps are added, what the tonic is and how a scale differs from a key.

Each round has 10 random questions drawn from a bank of 19. You need 80% correct to pass, which means at least 8 out of 10. Afterwards you can review all your answers.

Want to brush up first? Go to the Scales and Keys course, for example Part 2: The Major Scale, Part 5: Key Signatures or Part 6: The Circle of Fifths.
