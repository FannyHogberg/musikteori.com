Note Names Quiz

Natural notes, whole and half steps, sharps and flats.

About this quiz

This quiz tests note names and accidentals. You will name the natural notes on the white piano keys, fill in the missing note in a sequence and decide whether two notes are a whole step or a half step apart. You will also get questions about sharps, flats and natural signs, for example what a raised C or a lowered D is called.

Each round has 6 random questions drawn from a bank of 24. You need 60% correct to pass, and when you are done you can review your answers.

Consider reviewing Part 1: Note Names and Part 5: Sharps and Flats in the course Music Theory from Scratch before you start.
