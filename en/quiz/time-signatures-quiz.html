Time Signatures Quiz

How many beats fit in a measure of 4/4, 3/4 or 2/4 time?

About this quiz

This quiz is about beats, measures and time signatures. You will match time signatures such as 4/4, 3/4 and 2/4 with the right number of beats, explain what the top and bottom numbers mean and what a bar line is used for. You will also count how many quarter notes, eighth notes or half notes fit in a measure and choose which notes can complete an unfinished measure.

Each round has 8 random questions drawn from a bank of 21. You need 60% correct to pass, and when you are done you can review your answers.

Want to brush up first? Read Part 8: Beats and Time Signatures in the course Music Theory from Scratch. Since time signatures build on note values, Part 6: Note Values can help too.
