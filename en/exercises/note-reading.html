Note Reading

Practice recognizing different notes

In this exercise you will see a note and choose the correct answer. You get immediate feedback and can practice as much as you want!

How it works:

  • A random note is displayed
  • Click on the correct note name
  • Got it right? The next note appears immediately!
  • Got it wrong? You see the correct answer before the next note
